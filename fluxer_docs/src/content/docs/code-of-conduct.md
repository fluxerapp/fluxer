The [Fluxer community guidelines](https://fluxer.com/guidelines) set the standard of conduct in the Fluxer repository. They apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commits, branch names and anything else you submit. They also apply to conduct outside the repository when it creates a safety risk for anyone taking part in it.

feedback.fluxer.com has its own [code of conduct](https://feedback.fluxer.com/conduct).

## Technical decisions

Maintainers may reject contributions, decline feature requests, close threads and give direct technical criticism. When they do any of these things, it must be about the work itself and follow the Fluxer community guidelines.

## Reporting a violation

Email [safety@fluxer.com](mailto:safety@fluxer.com) to report a violation of these rules. Tell us what happened, where it happened, who was involved and any links that might help us. Please don't post the report publicly in the thread where it happened.

Fluxer staff handle every report. We share details only with people who need them to investigate the report, enforce these rules, protect the safety of anyone at risk or meet a legal obligation. Even if we never give out your name, the circumstances of a report can still let others work out that you were the one who sent it.

If the report is about a maintainer, another member of staff handles it. If no staff member is independent of that maintainer, we'll tell you so. Whoever the report is about, you can also report the conduct to GitHub directly.

## What we can do

We may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, restrict who can take part or block accounts. What we do depends on the conduct, the risk it poses and any earlier violations, and serious conduct can lead to an immediate block. Action in the repository is separate from any action on a Fluxer account.
